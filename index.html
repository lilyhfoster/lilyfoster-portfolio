01 — Sculptural Accessory

Eternal Purse

Materials3D Printed TPU
Year2026
CourseMAT 111FS
WithLily Foster, Alexis Tucker, Acucar Viano, Nicole Aguirre
A functional bag constructed by both machine and hand.

The Eternal Purse is a 3D printed bag consisting of TPU panelsjoined together with metal rings and ribbon, with a canvas strap glued on. This was a group project with classmates Alexis Tucker, Acucar Viana, and Nicole Aguirre. The white panels were printed by me using an Original Prusa i3 MK3S+ printer.

The process involved lots of troubleshooting. Infill levels were changed, bed temperatures were adjusted, and pattern sizes were significantly altered in PrusaSlicer to allow printing to be possible. Original plans to print onto tulle fabric were scrapped after two failed attempts.

This bag matters because it is an example of the intersection of fashion and technology in an era where many industries are beginning to embrace new technologies.

02 — Editorial Zine

We Feel Pretty

FormatZine
Year2026
CourseSOC 118PC
ByLily Foster
A close read of the western beauty aisle in the era of cultural amalgamation.

We Feel Pretty is a zine created as a final project for a Sociology of Popular Culture class. It explores how western beauty brands have utilized cultural appropriation to sell products and build their branding. I made this on Canva, using images sourced from the internet.

This is important to me because the zine acts as a concise way to educate on an issue that frequently goes overlooked by beauty product consumers.
